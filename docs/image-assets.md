## Hero prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide editorial website hero photography for Sagar Live Fish, a premium Indian seafood market. Create one exquisitely art-directed photorealistic food still life, landscape 3:2. Overhead three-quarter view of a large round dark charcoal ceramic platter filled with crushed translucent ice, holding three whole fresh silver freshwater fish with natural dark scales, several fresh grey shell-on prawns and a small blue-green mud crab. Accents of cut lime, a few coriander sprigs and scattered black pepper. Plate sits on a deep navy-blue slate surface. Cool natural side lighting with soft warm highlights, realistic wet silver scales, extremely appetizing market freshness, high-end food editorial photography, rich texture, restrained and beautiful composition. Seafood and platter fill frame; predominantly navy, silver, sage and pale lime palette. Absolutely no text, letters, logos, labels, frames or watermarks. This is an illustrative brand image, no claims about a real shop.

## Catalogue prompt template

Each catalogue asset was generated separately using the template below, replacing SUBJECT with its exact subject text.

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: seafood catalogue illustrative product photograph. SUBJECT. Art-directed premium Indian fish market still life, realistic overhead food photography, seafood rests on a shallow bed of crushed translucent ice on a pale cool grey stone surface, tiny accent of one fresh lime wedge and a few green coriander leaves at the edge. Entire seafood clearly visible in centre with breathing room on all sides for a landscape card crop. Soft natural daylight, exquisite realistic detail, restrained ivory and sage colour palette. Image composition landscape 3:2, no plates, no packaging, no cooked food. No text, logo, letters or watermarks. Illustrative product image only.

Subjects:

- **catla:** Two beautiful whole fresh Catla carp fish, broad silver bodies and dark scales
- **korameenu:** Two whole fresh Indian striped snakehead murrel fish, long dark olive mottled bodies with characteristic broad heads
- **prawns:** A generous small mound of fresh raw grey shell-on prawns, natural translucent grey shells, visible antennae, no cooked pink shrimp
- **crabs:** One whole fresh Indian green mud crab with realistic large strong claws, natural dark green mottled shell
- **seelavathi:** Two whole fresh silver grass carp fish, long silver scaled bodies and darker backs
- **apollo:** A neat stack of four raw boneless white fish fillets, translucent pearl-white flesh
- **tuna:** Two thick raw tuna steaks, rich dark red flesh, no salmon
- **nethallu:** A small arranged pile of whole tiny silver anchovy fish with natural iridescent skin
